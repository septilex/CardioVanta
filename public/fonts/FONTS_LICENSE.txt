CardioVanta Font Substitutes

As of the V1 frontend implementation, the authorized Mayo Clinic fonts were unavailable. The following legally usable open-source substitutes have been downloaded and bundled locally.

1. Inter
Source: Google Fonts / Fontsource (https://fontsource.org/fonts/inter)
Designer: Rasmus Andersson
License: SIL Open Font License, 1.1

2. Newsreader
Source: Google Fonts / Fontsource (https://fontsource.org/fonts/newsreader)
Designer: Production Type
License: SIL Open Font License, 1.1

These fonts are used solely as typographic substitutes to approximate the visual character of the design references. They are not the Mayo Clinic proprietary fonts and must not be presented as such.
